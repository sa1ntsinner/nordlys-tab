## Left alone on purpose

- Product and browser names, theme names, sky names and store labels, because they identify actual choices.
- Short controls and links that already say what they do, such as “Add to your browser”, “Open the settings” and “Read the privacy policy”.
- The connected apps paragraph, because its examples and task behaviour are already clear and specific.
- Empty image alt text on decorative images, so screen readers do not announce them twice.
- The demo iframe title and all non-copy attributes, scripts and styles, as requested.

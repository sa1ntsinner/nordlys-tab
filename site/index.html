This is the real thing

The new tab below is Nordlys itself, running on this page. Change what you like; nothing is saved.The new tab below is Nordlys itself. Tap it to run it here; nothing you change is saved.

  1. Your favourite sites, in folders

    Put your sites in folders and drag them into the order you like. You can see where each one will go, and undo any change.

  2. A dashboard for your day

    Turn it on and your day sits above your folders: tasks, habits, a focus timer, notes, weather and clocks for other cities. Drag a card by its title, stretch it by its corner.

  3. One thing at a time

    Focus mode gives your task the whole page: a large timer, rain or ocean sounds made in your browser, and your list at the side.

  4. Profiles for work and home

    Keep a Work page and a Home page, each with its own sites and look, and switch in a click. Sync goes through your browser's own sync.

  5. Commands after the > sign

    The search box uses your browser's search engine. It also finds your sites as you type, does sums, and takes commands after a > sign.

Add to your browser

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and other browsers built on Chrome.

  1. Add it from your browser's store. There is nothing to sign up for.
  2. Open a new tab. Nordlys offers to bring in your bookmarks, or you can start fresh.
  3. Keep the change if your browser asks whether you meant to change your new tab.

Open source, on GitHub.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, and anyone can read the code. There's a "Buy me a coffee" link in settings if you'd like to help.

Do I need an account?

No. Nordlys keeps your things in your browser. If you turn on sync, it uses your browser's own sync, just as your bookmarks do.

Can I keep my bookmarks?

Yes. Nordlys offers to bring them in the first time you open a new tab. You can also import a bookmarks file from any browser, or keep one Nordlys folder in step with a folder of browser bookmarks.

Will it slow my computer down?

It shouldn't. The skies slow down when you're away and stop when the tab is hidden. You can also choose a still background.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and other browsers built on Chrome. The Firefox and Safari versions are in the stores' review.

What does it send over the internet?

Only what a feature you use needs: searches go to your search engine, icon lookups to an icon library, and weather to a free weather service for the city you choose. The privacy policy lists all of it.

Can I get my old new tab back?

Yes. Turn Nordlys off or remove it on your browser's extensions page, and your usual new tab comes back.

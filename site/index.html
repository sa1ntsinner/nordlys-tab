Getting started

Set it up in a few steps

  1. 1

    Add it to your browser

    Add Nordlys from your browser's store. It's free and you don't need an account.

  2. 2

    Bring in your bookmarks

    When you open a new tab for the first time, Nordlys offers to bring in your bookmarks. You can also start fresh.

  3. 3

    Set up your page

    Pick a sky and colours, move folders where you like them, and turn on the dashboard if you want one.

What it does

What you can do on a new tab

Keep your favourite sites in folders

Put your sites in folders, then drag them into the order you like. You can see where each one will go, and undo any change.

  • Icons for familiar sites
  • Folders you can tuck away
  • Fits the whole page on one screen

A dashboard you can turn on

If you want more than bookmarks, turn on the dashboard. It puts cards for your day above your folders. Drag a card by its title, or pull its corner to make it bigger.

  • Add a task by typing "call mum tomorrow"
  • Habits, notes and a countdown
  • Weather and clocks for other cities

Focus mode, for one thing at a time

Focus mode gives your task the whole page, with a large timer, rain or ocean sounds, and your list at the side. You can keep a small timer above your other tabs, too.

  • Pomodoro or a simple count-up
  • Sounds made in your browser
  • See how long you've focused today

Your tasks from the apps you already use

If your to-do list lives in Todoist, GitHub, Trello or another tool, connect it and your tasks show up on the dashboard. Tick one off here and it's ticked off there, too. Calendars work with any calendar link.

Nordlys connects to each app from your browser. Your sign-in stays on your computer.

Private by default

Nordlys needs no account and tracks nothing. Your bookmarks and settings are kept in your browser. If you turn on sync, it uses your browser's own sync. Weather and icons go online when you ask for them.

  • No account
  • No analytics
  • Open source

Questions

Things people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads or paid plan, and anyone can read the code. There's a "Buy me a coffee" link in settings if you'd like to help.

Do I need an account?

No. Nordlys keeps your things in your browser. If you turn on sync, it uses your browser's own sync, just as your bookmarks do.

Can I keep my bookmarks?

Yes. Nordlys offers to bring them in the first time you open a new tab. You can also import a bookmarks file from any browser or keep one Nordlys folder in step with a folder of browser bookmarks.

Will it slow my computer down?

It shouldn't. The skies slow down when you're away and stop when the tab is hidden. You can also choose a still background.

Which browsers does it work in?

It works now in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and other browsers built on Chrome. Firefox and Safari (Mac) versions are in store review.

What does it send over the internet?

Only when you use a feature that needs it. Searches go to your chosen search engine, icon lookups to an icon library, and weather requests to a free weather service for your chosen city. The privacy policy lists what's sent.

Can I get my old new tab back?

Yes. Turn Nordlys off or remove it from your browser's extensions page. Your usual new tab will come back.

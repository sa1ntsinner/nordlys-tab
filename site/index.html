A quiet sky behind every new tab

Nordlys replaces Chrome's new tab page with your bookmarks, in folders you arrange yourself, over a sky drawn on your own machine. It has no account and no analytics.

What it does

Arrange

Folders go where you put them

Drag a folder between two others, or between rows to start a new one, and the board shows where it will land before you let go. Fitted makes every row run edge to edge.

Bookmark size, spacing and the width of the board are set in one panel, with the board itself as the preview and Undo for every step.

Icons

Icons that look like the site

Search a brand by name and Nordlys offers a clean vector, then keeps it on your machine. Chrome's own favicon, a picture from any address, a file or a single letter work too.

An icon taken from a web address remembers it, and the last few before it, so you can go back to one.

Time of day

A sky that keeps your hours

Turn on Follow the time of day and every scene is lit by where the sun is for you: warm at sunrise and sunset, pale by day, with stars after dark.

Nordlys works it out from the date and your time zone, so it never asks where you are.

Themes

21 themes, light and dark

A theme sets the background, the glass and the colours of the sky behind it, so Gruvbox gets an amber sky and OLED Obsidian stays black.

The sky behind the clock and the search box is measured as it moves and held back just enough to keep the text readable. If none of the 21 fit, the theme studio makes one from three colours.

A search box that answers

Type 45 * 12 + sqrt(144) and the answer appears under the box. Enter sends your search to the engine you chose in Chrome.

Commands

Start with > and the board takes instructions, like theme nord or move YouTube to Daily. You see the result before you press Enter.

Folders that follow Chrome

Point a folder at one of your Chrome bookmark folders and it keeps up with it. Nordlys asks to read bookmarks only when you do this.

Six scenes

Nordlys, Halo, Silk, Frost, Contour and Fjord, or your own picture or looping video. Still mode paints one frame and stops.

Eight languages

English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Chinese and Turkish.

Your bookmarks, portable

Bring bookmarks in from any browser as HTML, and take everything back out as HTML or JSON whenever you like.

Your setup stays on your computer

Settings and bookmarks live in Chrome's local storage. There is no account, no analytics and no code loaded from anywhere else.

Nordlys goes online only when you ask it to. Icon search sends the name you typed to Iconify after you press Search, and an image address you paste is fetched so you can preview it. The search box sends nothing until you press Enter.

Try it on your next new tab

Nordlys works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

Add to Chrome Download the source

  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the folder.
